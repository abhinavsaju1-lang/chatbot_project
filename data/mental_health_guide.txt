MindSense Mental Health & Counseling Knowledge Base

1. Emotion & Stress Assessment Guidelines:
- Stress: Characterized by feelings of being overwhelmed, muscle tension, fatigue, or racing thoughts. Recommended interventions include deep breathing exercises, mindfulness, and cognitive reframing.
- Anxiety: Excessive worry, restlessness, heightened heart rate, or fear of future events. Recommended interventions include grounding techniques (5-4-3-2-1 sensory method), structured daily routines, and progressive muscle relaxation.
- Sadness & Depression: Persistent feelings of hopelessness, low energy, loss of interest in activities, or sleep disturbances. Recommended interventions include behavioral activation, encouraging gentle physical movement, supportive active listening, and social reconnection.
- Anger & Frustration: Emotional reactions to perceived unfairness, feeling blocked, or boundary violations. Recommended interventions include emotional validation, pause/timeout strategies, and constructive expression.

2. Empathetic Counseling Principles:
- Active Listening: Reflect the user's emotional state with validating phrasing ("It sounds like you are carrying a lot of weight right now.").
- Non-Clinical Support: Provide compassionate, non-judgmental guidance while maintaining safety boundaries.
- Safety & Crisis Protocol: If a user expresses self-harm or severe crisis thoughts, immediately provide helpline resources and strongly recommend seeking emergency professional help.

3. Self-Care & Coping Strategies:
- Daily Mindfulness: 5-minute focused breathing sessions.
- Sleep Hygiene: Maintaining consistent sleep schedules and reducing screen exposure before bedtime.
- Physical Exercise: Light walking or stretching to boost endorphin levels.
